# LEVL Product Strategy 2026

## Positioning

LEVL is a personal progression system for people who know what they want to
change but struggle to turn intention into consistent real-world action.

The product promise is:

> LEVL turns a goal into one concrete action, helps the user complete it,
> verifies the result, and makes the change visible in their world and
> character.

LEVL must not feel like another habit tracker, task list, AI chat, or social
network. AI is an invisible engine for adaptation, not the product headline.

## Core Daily Loop

1. Open LEVL and see one primary move for today.
2. Start the action with a verification method that fits the task.
3. Complete the action and leave an appropriate trace: time, place, text,
   image, link, or self-confirmation.
4. See an immediate consequence in the Life Map and character state.
5. Return to see the next move and the accumulated weekly change.

## Five Required Product Pillars

1. **Personal direction**: generated actions follow the user's real goal,
   capacity, context, and rejection history.
2. **Verified action**: completion records a real result instead of only a
   checkbox.
3. **Visible consequence**: every confirmed action changes the route,
   character, or an earned detail.
4. **Return loop**: one useful daily system contact and a meaningful weekly
   recap.
5. **Safe social proof**: friends can support progress without exposing exact
   tasks, sensitive data, or live coordinates.

## MVP Scope Decisions

- Keep the dashboard focused on one primary action and at most two alternatives.
- Use one task system with contextual verification instead of separate complex
  modes and screens.
- Ship a high-quality 2.5D full-body avatar pilot before a large clothing
  catalogue or real-time 3D.
- Start social with nickname, friend code, private progress profile, and
  supportive reactions.
- Do not ship exact live player locations or an open chat in the first social
  version. They create privacy, moderation, safety, and empty-network risks.
- Do not sell AI as the main value. Sell real movement and visible progress.

## Delivery Order

1. Deploy quest generation v2.5 and location verification migration.
2. Complete the action -> verification -> visible consequence loop.
3. Add product analytics and crash monitoring for the daily loop.
4. Add weekly recap and return-after-absence experience.
5. Produce one full-body 2.5D base, five views, and one clothing set.
6. Add nickname, friend code, private friend profile, and reactions.
7. Expand map events and customization only after retention data supports it.

## Success Metrics

- Onboarding completion rate.
- Share of users who start their first action on day 0.
- Share of started actions that are verified.
- Day 1 and Day 7 retained users.
- Weekly recap open rate.
- Average verified actions per active week.
- Quest rejection rate and the share of replacements subsequently completed.

## Current Product Priority

The verified-action loop, private analytics, weekly recap, full-body character
studio, and the first reward-gated wardrobe are implemented.

The highest-impact next slice is reward inventory v1: achievements grant owned
items through an explicit inventory, the character studio explains the unlock
condition, and equipping an item persists across sessions and devices. This
turns customization into a visible consequence of real progress instead of a
standalone editor.

After that, build the release foundation (production application identifiers,
signing, privacy declarations, account deletion, crash reporting, and shared
subscription entitlements), then add the private social foundation. Live player
locations and open chat remain outside the first release.
